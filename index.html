About meOm mig

Hi, I'm Tiffany Schilling, a Multimedia Design student at Zealand in Slagelse, specializing in frontend development. I'm particularly interested in programming and building functional, user-friendly websites where design and technology work together. Outside my studies, I enjoy working on personal projects and exploring new technologies.

Hej, jeg hedder Tiffany Schilling, og jeg læser multimediedesign på Zealand i Slagelse med fokus på frontend-udvikling. Jeg interesserer mig især for programmering og for at skabe funktionelle, brugervenlige websites, hvor design og teknik arbejder sammen. Ved siden af studiet arbejder jeg også på mine egne projekter og bruger gerne tid på at lære nye teknologier.

My background

My interest in programming started in high school (HTX), where I worked with Python, C++, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. After graduating, I studied Game Development and Learning Technology at SDU, gaining experience with C#, Unity, Java, MongoDB, and R. During my studies, I realised that web development and programming were the areas I wanted to pursue.

This led me to study Multimedia Design. I started at UCL in Odense before transferring to Zealand in Slagelse. Throughout my studies, I have worked with both the design process and technical website development, including user research, prototyping, responsive layouts, and frontend programming.

I primarily work with HTML, CSS, Sass, and JavaScript, and I also have experience with Vue, PHP, API integration, and databases such as MariaDB and Firebase. I particularly enjoy developing the functionality behind websites and finding solutions that work well technically while making sense to the user. Recently, I have been focusing on Vue and database integration through my exam project, Bookshelf.
